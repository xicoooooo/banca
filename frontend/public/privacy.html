Banca

Privacy

Last updated 7 October 2026

Banca is a personal, open source project: card games played with virtual chips against a computer opponent. Chips are free, cannot be bought, cannot be cashed out and have no value. Banca never asks for or handles money or payment details.

What is kept when you play as a guest

  • A display name, which starts as "Guest" and a number until you change it.
  • Your chip balance and the record of every change to it.
  • A record of each round you play: the game, what you staked, the result, and a summary of how you played it, such as whether you folded or doubled down. Your statistics, level and achievements are worked out from these.
  • A random token, stored in your browser, that tells the server which profile is yours. The server keeps only a scrambled copy of it.
  • A few notes that stay in your browser and are never sent anywhere: whether sound is on, which of Banca's guides to the games you have already seen, whether you have turned down the offer to install, and your choice of session reminder with the time your current sitting began. If you install Banca, your device also keeps a copy of the app's own files so that it can open without a network. None of your game data is in that copy.

What is added when you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. It saves your profile to an account so you can reach it from another device. Sign-in is handled by Supabase, which keeps your Google account's email address, name and profile picture as part of that account. Banca's own game records hold only the account's identifier, and your first name if you had not chosen a display name. Banca never sees your Google password and asks Google for nothing beyond this basic profile.

What other players can see

At a shared table or in a shared room, including a private one you open or are invited to, the other players there see your display name, the chips you put down, your cards where the game is played with cards face up, and how each round went for you. If you signed in with Google and had not chosen a name, that name is your first name; you can change it on your profile at any time. Anything you type in a room's chat is shown, under your display name, to the players in that room, so please do not put personal details in it. Links are removed and the most offensive words are starred out automatically, and any player can mute another. Nobody reads the chat on Banca's behalf. What is said in a room is kept only while the server is running and is not saved.

If you sign in, you take a place in the weekly leagues. Your display name, your league, how many rounds you played and what you won at the tables appear on the leaderboards, which anyone visiting Banca can see, signed in or not. Each name there opens that player's public page, which shows their display name, the month they joined, their level, their league, how many rounds they have played, how many achievements they have earned, and the trophies they have won by finishing in the top three of a league, each with its week. Players who stay guests are never listed and have no public page. Nothing else about you is shown: not your email, your balance or your history.

If you sign in, you can also have friends. You get a code to give out, and someone who types it can ask to be your friend; nothing happens unless you accept, and either of you can end it at any time. A friend sees your display name, your league, whether you are on Banca at the moment, and which table you are sitting at if it is one anyone can join. They do not see your balance, your history or how you play. Whether you are online is worked out from your being connected and is not recorded: Banca keeps no log of when you were here.

What it is used for

Only to run the game: to keep your chips and history, to show you your own statistics, and to let you sign in. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used to profile you. There are no advertising or analytics trackers on this site.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase hosts the database and the sign-in accounts, in the European Union.
  • Render and Vercel host the server and the website, and keep ordinary technical logs such as IP addresses.
  • Groq runs the language model that plays the opponent and, when you ask it, coaches you. It is sent the state of the hand being played, such as the cards on the table and the bets made, and nothing that identifies you.
  • Google, if you choose to sign in with it.

Your choices

You can play without signing in. You can change your display name at any time on your profile. To have your profile and account deleted, or to ask what is held about you, write to faragaodias@gmail.com and it will be done.
